**Coral Bay Heat** — an open-world crime sandbox that plays in a desktop browser from its own static files. You are Mari Solano, back in the port city of Coral Bay and working off a favour for fixer Marlin Doss: steal a Zephyr GT, run a hot package across town, crash six gates against the clock, work a borrowed cab, and total the Vipers' rides — then keep hustling on Marlin's endless job board, which also deals in stranded contacts who need a lift and Viper drivers who need running down. The city lives around you: traffic that keeps its lanes, pedestrians who stroll, flee, mug-worthy wallets and hail cabs, and a witness-based five-star wanted system — the CBPD intercept, ram, box in and set roadblocks, and pin you for a bust if you let them. A living bay with moored boats, gulls, a boardwalk and beach umbrellas, rooftops with water tanks and shopfront awnings, a full day cycle from morning glare to a blue night of lit windows and headlamps, synthesized engine, siren and crash audio, a minimap with police blips and the hour on it, and a save that remembers your money, your story and your pistol. Vanilla Canvas 2D ES modules, zero dependencies, every name, sprite and sound original and generated in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to walk and drive, E in/out of cars,
# F to mug, Shift to sprint, Space for the handbrake, Esc to pause.
```

Hand the entry in (writes `out/` with the game, the screenshots and dossier.json):

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
